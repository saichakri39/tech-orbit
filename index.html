ABOUT US

One club. Many possibilities.

Tech Orbit is a student-focused technology club created to bring curious minds together. We encourage students to explore new technologies, strengthen practical skills, work in teams, and transform ideas into projects.

Through workshops, technical sessions, project building, competitions, and peer learning, the club creates a space where students can learn beyond the classroom.

◉

OUR VISION

Build a community of creators.

To build a collaborative community where students explore technology, develop practical skills, and turn innovative ideas into meaningful solutions.

✦

OUR MISSION

Learn. Experiment. Collaborate.

To provide students with opportunities to learn, experiment, collaborate, and build through workshops, technical events, projects, and peer learning.
