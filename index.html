ABOUT US

One club. Many possibilities.

Tech Orbit is a student-focused technology club created to bring curious minds together. We encourage students to explore new technologies, strengthen practical skills, work in teams, and transform ideas into projects.

Through technical sessions, ideathons, project building, competitions, and peer learning, the club creates a space where students can learn beyond the classroom.

◉

VISION

Learning together. Growing together.

A place where AI & Data Science students stop learning in isolation and start learning from each other — building real skill, real confidence, and a habit of never stopping at “good enough.”

✦

MISSION

Learn. Build. Compete. Lead.

  • Get 2nd, 3rd, and final-year students learning from each other, not just from a syllabus.
  • Run weekly, practical sessions that teach things students will actually use.
  • Put students on real projects and challenges, not just theory.
  • Build leadership and problem-solving alongside technical skill.
  • Push students to compete, build, and create work that matters.
